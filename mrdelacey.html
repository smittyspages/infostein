Description

Mr. Delacey is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. Mr. Delacey is a kind man who is blind and lives in a cottage with his son Felix and daughter Agatha. He is the first person the creature speaks with, and Mr. Delacey is the only person who shows the creature kindness and compassion.

Music Choice

The song we chose for Mr. Delacey is "Heal the World" by Michael Jackson. We believe it shows his kindness towards the creature, and we think it could very well represent his outlook on the world.

Quote

“I am blind, and cannot judge your form.”

We believe this quote captures Mr. Delacey's compassionate view on people and the world.

Character Traits

  • Nonjudgmental.
  • Compassionate.
  • Carefree.
