Mary Shelley

Mary Shelley was born in 1797 in England. She is an author, and is considered one of the earliest writers of science fiction. Her most famous novels are "Frankenstein" and "The Modern Prometheus."

Gris Grimly

Gris Grimly was born in 1969 in the United States. He is an illustrator and graphic novelist, his most famous works are graphic novel adaptations of classic literature.
