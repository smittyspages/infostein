Description

Felix Delacey is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. Felix is a minor side character. He is the son of Mr. Delacey and the brother of Agatha. Felix is a judgemental person who is quick to judge the creature based on his appearance.
