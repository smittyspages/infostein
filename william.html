William Frankenstein

Description

William Frankenstein is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. William Frankenstein is a side character, but he has a significant role in the story. William is Victor's younger brother, and is the first victim of The Creature.

Music Choice

The song we chose for William Frankenstein is "Sign of the Times" by Harry Styles. We believe it shows his innocence and naivety.

Character Traits

  • Innocent.
  • Naive.
  • Kind.
