Professor Waldman

Description

Professor Waldman is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. He is a professor at the university where Victor Frankenstein studies. Waldman is known for his enthusiasm for science and his influence on Victor's early education.

Music Choice

The song we chose for Victor Frankenstein is "Scientist" by Coldplay. We believe it represents his scientific curiosity and passion, but also the consequences of knowledge.

Quote

“Knowledge is power.”

We believe this quote reflects Waldman's philosophy and his influence on Victor, while still showcasing the strength of knowledge.
