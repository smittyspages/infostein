Victor Frankenstein

Description

Victor Frankenstein is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. Victor is the main character, Elizabeth Lavenza's husband, Henry Clerval's bestfriend, the brothers of William and the creator of The Creature

Music Choice

The song we chose for Victor Frankenstein is "Sound of Silence" by Simon & Garfunkel. We believe it shows his isolation and inner turmoil.

Quote

"Nothing is more painful to the human mind, than, after the feelings have been worked up by a quick succession of events, the dead calmness of innaction and certainty which follows, and deprives the soul both of hope and fear."

Character Traits

  • Victor tends to run away from his problems.
  • Revengeful.
  • Intelligent.
