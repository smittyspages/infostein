Professor Krempe

Description

Professor Krempe is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. Professor Krempe is a stern and unforgiving professor at the university where Victor Frankenstein studies.

Music Choice

The song we chose for Professor Krempe is "Requiem" by Mozart. We believe it reflects his fancy posture and dismissive nature.

Quote

“Have you then really spent your time studying such nonsense?”

We believe this quote captures Professor Krempe's strict and dismissive attitude towards Victor's studies.

Character Traits

  • Strict.
  • Discouraging.
  • Intolerant.
