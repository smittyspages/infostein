Elizabeth Lavenza

Description

Elizabeth Lavenza is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. Elizabeth Lavenza is one of the main characters.Elizabeth is Victor's wife and main love interest in the story.

Music Choice

The song we chose for Elizabeth is "Here Comes the Sun" by The Beatles. We believe it reflects her optimistic, gentle, and hopeful nature.

Character Traits

  • Kindhearted.
  • Compassionate.
  • Patient.
