Henry Clerval

Description

Henry Clerval is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. Henry Clerval is one of the main characters. Clerval is Victor's best friend, and the man to bring Victor out of his depression.

Music Choice

The song we chose for Henry Clerval is "What a Wonderful World" by Louis Armstrong. We believe it truly captures his compassionate nature, and view on the world.
