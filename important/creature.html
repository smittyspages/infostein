The Creature

Description

The Creature is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. The Creature is the main protagonist, and plays a vital role in the story, and is the creation of Victor Frankenstein.

Music Choice

The song we chose for The Creature is "Creep" by Radiohead. We believe it reflects his feelings of isolation, alienation, and longing for acceptance.

Character Traits

  • Lonely.
  • Intelligent.
  • Misunderstood.
  • Remorseful.
