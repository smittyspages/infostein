Description

Beaufort is an original character by Mary Shelley. Gris Grimly illustrates the character in his graphic version of the novel. Victor is the main character, Elizabeth Lavenza's husband, Henry Clerval's bestfriend, the brothers of William and the creator of The Creature

Music Choice

The song we chose for Beaufort is "Boulevard of Broken Dreams" by Green day. We believe this song reflects his emotional, social and financial downfall.

Quote

“Caroline, your kindness is all I have left in this world.”

We believe this quote encapsulates Beaufort's true love for his daughter and fatherly affection.
